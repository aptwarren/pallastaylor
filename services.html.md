# Services | Pallas Taylor

Event support packages from Pallas Taylor, for lean investment teams planning breakfasts, happy hours, LP gatherings and more. Pallas Taylor can own the whole event or step into the pieces a team needs help carrying.

## 01 - Breakfast Briefing

For intimate investor, founder or team breakfasts that need a calm, polished start.

Includes: one planning call; run-of-show and day-of timeline; venue and vendor coordination; guest-list and RSVP tracking; up to 3 hours of onsite support; post-event wrap-up.

Scope: designed for up to 30 guests. Food, venue and vendor costs are separate.

Outcome: guests arrive to a room that is ready, and the team gets to focus on the conversation.

## 02 - Happy Hour / LP Gathering

For polished relationship-building events without adding work to the internal team.

Includes: planning kickoff and event brief; venue and vendor coordination; invitation copy and RSVP tracking; run-of-show; one lead onsite operator for up to 5 hours; guest arrival, vendor management and event closeout.

Scope: designed for up to 75 guests. Third-party costs are separate.

Outcome: a thoughtful gathering that feels easy for guests and does not become a second job for the team.

## 03 - White-Glove Event Partner

For higher-touch LP dinners, portfolio gatherings and multi-vendor events where Pallas Taylor owns the details end to end.

Includes: event concept and planning roadmap; venue sourcing support; vendor sourcing, negotiation and coordination; invitation and guest-management support; detailed production timeline and run-of-show; lead operator plus staffing plan; up to 8 hours onsite; post-event reconciliation and debrief.

Scope: custom scope based on guest count, production and staffing.

Outcome: one trusted partner carrying the event from first idea through final closeout.

## 04 - Event Sprint

For teams that already have a plan but need an experienced operator to rescue, finish or run it.

Good for: vendor follow-up; production timelines; guest lists and RSVPs; setup coordination; day-of execution.

Scope: four-hour minimum. Scope is agreed before work begins.

Outcome: focused support for the exact part of the event that is taking too much time or needs an experienced hand.
