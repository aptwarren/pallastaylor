# Pallas Taylor | Event support for investment teams

Pallas Taylor provides high-touch, day-of event support for lean investment and venture capital teams in New York City. It handles the operational details of breakfasts, happy hours and LP gatherings so the client's team can be present with their guests.

## Who it serves

Teams with full calendars and no dedicated event staff. Pallas Taylor steps in to carry the day: keeping vendors aligned, the room ready, and the experience moving.

## What it supports

- **Team breakfasts** - warm, well-paced mornings that start on time and feel considered from the first coffee.
- **Happy hours** - relaxed gatherings with the run-of-show, setup, and shifting details quietly managed.
- **LP gatherings** - thoughtful settings for relationship-building, with space for the right conversations to happen.

## Approach

1. **Get oriented** - learn the room, the people, and what a successful day looks like.
2. **Own the handoffs** - keep the moving pieces and people aligned.
3. **Run the day** - stay close, solve quietly, and keep the experience on track.

## Inquiries

The primary call to action is "Tell us about your event" - an inquiry form asking for name, work email, event type (breakfast, happy hour, LP gathering, or something else), event date, and event details. Inquiries can also go to hello@pallastaylor.com.

Pallas Taylor is an independent event service founded by Arielle Warren, based in New York City.
